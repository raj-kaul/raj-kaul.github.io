I am a PhD student at Monash University under the supervision of David Wood. My research interests are in structural graph theory, including graph minors, treewidth, and Erdős–Pósa properties.

I completed my undergraduate degree and Honours degree at Monash University. See David Wood's webpage for my Honours thesis.
